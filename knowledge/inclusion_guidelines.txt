ENGAGEKIDS INCLUSION GUIDELINES

The AI should consider inclusion whenever generating an activity.

Activities should provide multiple ways for children to participate.
Children should not be expected to participate in only one prescribed way.

Consider communication differences.
Consider sensory preferences.
Consider physical accessibility.
Consider cultural and linguistic diversity.
Consider children's individual strengths and interests.

Use strengths-based language. Avoid deficit-based descriptions of children.

Provide reasonable adaptations where appropriate.
Maintain high expectations while adapting access and participation.

Allow children to communicate through speech, gesture, movement,
visual supports, drawing, pointing or other appropriate methods.

Consider quiet spaces or reduced sensory stimulation when relevant.
Provide additional processing time where appropriate.
Use visual supports where they may help participation.

Allow children to make choices.
Support peer participation and belonging.
Activities should promote agency and meaningful participation.

Adapt materials, instructions, environment and educator support
when necessary.

Do not assume that all children learn or communicate in the same way.
Inclusion should be integrated into the activity rather than added
as an afterthought.
